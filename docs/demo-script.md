# 60-second demo script (screen recording)

Record with QuickTime (File → New Screen Recording) or Loom. Keep the mouse slow.
Target: 60–75 seconds, no voice needed if you add captions; a Loom with voice is
better for Upwork.

1. **0:00** — Dashboard, empty or with the demo batch already loaded.
   Say: "This is the lead pipeline I built for the brief — every channel in, scored
   and routed automatically."
2. **0:08** — Click **Run demo batch**. Ten leads appear one by one.
   Point at the stat cards: hot leads, average score, follow-ups queued.
3. **0:20** — Click the **Hot** tab.
   Say: "Two of ten were hot. Sarah has a $15–25k budget and asked for a proposal —
   the model routed her to a rep with a 15-minute SLA."
4. **0:30** — Open **Sarah Mitchell**. Scroll slowly through:
   - score + next action,
   - "Why this score" evidence bullets,
   - the original message,
   - CRM record id,
   - follow-up plan (auto-reply already sent, day-1 and day-3 scheduled).
5. **0:50** — Click **Fast-forward 1 day** → the day-1 follow-up sends, the counter
   updates. Say: "Follow-ups are real, not a static list."
6. **0:60** — Back to the table, filter **Cold**, open the SEO spam lead.
   Say: "Spam is archived with a reason, so nobody wastes time on it."

Optional closing shot: edit `CRM_WEBHOOK_URL` in `.env` and show the log line
`crm_synced` — "plugging in their CRM is one environment variable."

## Things not to claim

- Don't say the demo is running GPT-4o if no key is configured; the badge shows
  the active engine.
- Don't promise the 6–8 hour → 2 minute improvement as measured; it is the target.
